# Remote Work IT Checklist
Before working from home: connect to the VPN, confirm you can reach the shared drive and test a Teams call.
Use a private Wi-Fi network with a strong password. Avoid public Wi-Fi unless the VPN is on.
Keep the laptop updated and locked when not in use. Do not let family members use the work laptop.
Printing at home is not supported for confidential documents.
Internet problems at home: restart the router, move closer to it, and check whether other devices are also affected. If only work apps fail, flush the DNS cache and reconnect the VPN.
If the VPN fails with authentication errors, your password may have expired.
