# How to Raise a Good Support Ticket
A clear ticket is solved faster. Include:
1. What you were trying to do.
2. What happened instead, including the exact error message.
3. When it started and whether it ever worked.
4. How many people are affected.
5. What you already tried.
6. A screenshot or log file; use the paperclip in the chat to attach it.
Pick the right type: use an incident when something is broken and a service request when you need something new, such as software or access.
Do not raise duplicate tickets; add a comment to the existing ticket instead. Rate the support when your ticket is closed.
