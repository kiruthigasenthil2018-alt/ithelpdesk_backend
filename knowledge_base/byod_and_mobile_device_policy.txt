# BYOD and Mobile Device Policy
Personal phones may be used for company email and Teams after enrolling in device management.
Requirements: a screen lock PIN or biometric, the latest operating system updates, and no jailbreak or root.
Lost or stolen phone: report it to the helpdesk immediately. IT can remotely remove company data. Remote wipe and device quarantine are consequential actions that need approval.
Company data must not be copied to personal cloud storage or personal email.
Leaving the company: company data is removed from your personal device on your last day.
