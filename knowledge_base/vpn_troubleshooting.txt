# VPN Troubleshooting Guide
If the VPN will not connect, first check that your internet connection works by opening any website.
Step 1: Close the VPN client completely and reopen it.
Step 2: Check that the date and time on your laptop are correct. A wrong clock breaks VPN certificates.
Step 3: Disconnect from other VPN or proxy software, then try again.
Step 4: Flush the DNS cache (ipconfig /flushdns on Windows) and restart the network adapter.
Step 5: If the error says "authentication failed", your password may have expired. See the password reset guide.
Step 6: If the VPN profile is corrupted, ask the helpdesk to re-issue the VPN profile.
If none of these steps work, escalate to Network Operations with the error message and a screenshot.
