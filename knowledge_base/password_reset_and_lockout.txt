# Password Reset and Account Lockout
Accounts lock automatically after 5 failed login attempts and unlock after 30 minutes.
To reset your password use the self-service portal and verify with multi-factor authentication (MFA).
If you lost your MFA device, the helpdesk must verify your identity before resetting MFA.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and expire every 90 days.
Resetting a password or unlocking an account is a consequential action and requires human approval from IT staff.
